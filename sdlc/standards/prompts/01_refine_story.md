# 1. Refine a story

Refine @sdlc/backlog/unrefined/ST-XXX.md against @sdlc/standards/definition_of_ready.md.

## Expected outcome

- Keep the story intent intact.
- Make requirements concrete, testable, and small enough for one iteration.
- Identify the known business context, dependencies, and explicit out-of-scope items.

## Constraints

- Do **not** invent requirements or make product decisions.
- Ask focused questions for each gap that prevents the story from meeting the Definition of Ready.
- Record unanswered questions in the refined story as **Open questions**.
- Do **not** modify the original ticket.

## Deliverable

Save the refined story as @sdlc/backlog/refined/ST-XXX.md.
